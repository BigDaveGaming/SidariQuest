READ THIS FIRST

You are making the art for Chapter 1 of "Big Dave & Gobby Sharon's Sidari
Quest", a phone game that teaches practical Greek.

STYLE REFERENCE: reference/style_reference_title_screen.png
Every image must match that picture: the same bright, painted cartoon style,
chunky outlines, saturated sunny colours, exaggerated comedy faces, and
the SAME Dave and Sharon (same faces, bodies, hair, tattoos, outfits).

Please make the images ONE AT A TIME, in the order listed, and use the
exact file names given so they slot straight into the game.

*** DELIVERY: ALL IMAGES MUST COME BACK AS ONE ZIP FILE ***
When every image is done, put them all into a single zip file called
SidariQuest_Chapter1_Art.zip, organised into these folders:

  SidariQuest_Chapter1_Art/
    title/        (A1–A3)
    characters/   (B1–B2, C1–C7, D1–D7)
    locals/       (E1–E12)
    backgrounds/  (F1–F4)
    intro/        (G1–G3)
    extras/       (H1–H2)

- Use the EXACT file names from this brief (lowercase, underscores, .png).
- PNG only. Keep transparency on the cut-outs.
- Include a file called CHECKLIST.txt in the zip listing every file and
  marking any that are missing, so nothing gets lost.
- If you can't fit all 40 in one go, make the zip in batches
  (e.g. SidariQuest_Chapter1_Art_part1.zip, _part2.zip) using the same
  folder structure, and say which images are in each part.

GENERAL RULES
1. No real brand names or logos anywhere (no real shops, bars, car
   companies, sportswear or designer logos).
2. LEAVE ALL SHOP SIGNS AND STREET SIGNS BLANK unless told otherwise.
   The game adds the Greek text itself. Plain blank boards/panels only.
3. Do NOT add any buttons, menus, speech bubbles, captions or UI.
4. Cut-outs (characters) = transparent background PNG, nothing else in
   the image, whole figure/bust visible, not cropped at the edges.
5. Backgrounds = NO characters in them. Portrait 1024 x 1536.
   Keep the bottom third fairly simple (floor, counter, pavement) because
   a dialogue box sits there.
6. Dave and Sharon face slightly towards the RIGHT of the image.
   All locals face slightly towards the LEFT (so they face each other
   on screen).
7. Face images (busts) must all use identical framing for each character:
   head and shoulders, same size, same position, so the game can swap
   them without the character jumping around.

THE CHARACTERS
==================================================

BIG DAVE (playable)
Big stocky British bloke, 50s. Bald, slightly sunburnt head, grey stubble
beard. Sunglasses pushed up on his head. Yellow-and-blue palm-tree vest,
thick gold chain, gold watch, tribal tattoos on both arms and shoulders.
Board shorts, white socks, sliders. Smug, confident. Exactly as in the
style reference.

GOBBY SHARON (playable)
British woman, 40s/50s. Bright copper-ginger hair in a messy high bun with
loose strands, sunglasses on her head. Heavy dark drawn-on eyebrows, huge
false lashes, big glossy lips. Large gold hoop earrings, gold bangle,
long pink nails. Leopard-print bikini top with a sarong/shorts, leopard
print phone case, phone always in hand. Loud, gesturing. Exactly as in the
style reference.

SPYROS RENT-A-VAN (local, the van hire man)
Greek man, 40s, slim, neat moustache, aviator sunglasses on his collar.
Plain navy polo shirt (blank, no logo), car keys on a lanyard, clipboard.
A man who has seen what tourists do to vans. Nervous energy.

KYRIA ELENI (local, landlady)
Greek grandmother (yiayia), 70s, small and round, grey hair in a bun,
black dress with a floral apron, gold cross necklace. Kind but sees
everything. Often holding a bowl of figs.

KYRIOS SPYROS (local, landlord, Eleni's husband)
Greek grandfather, 70s, tall and thin, white bushy moustache, flat cap,
short-sleeved checked shirt, trousers with braces, worry beads
(komboloi) in one hand. Inspects everything. Holds the deposit.

FAT SPYROS (local, runs the mini market)
Greek man, 50s, very round, cheerful, balding with a comb-over, white
vest under an open short-sleeved shirt, pencil behind his ear. Lives
behind the till.
