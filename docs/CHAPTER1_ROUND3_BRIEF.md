## Update after playtesting (Mike, 20 Nov 2026) — supersedes sections 3–4 where they differ

- **The core loop is the original one:** say the wrong thing → "You just said…" → the
  local's reply and the joke (Dave/Sharon chipping in) → **Try again**. Don't show the
  correct answer before the retry.
- **Phrase cards are optional and subtle.** No forced cards and no quick checks in the story.
  The first time a phrase comes up, the 📖 button glows with "New phrase". Getting it right
  shows "📖 Added to your phrasebook" (tap to see the card).
- **Card tips are Dave's notes written before the trip**: about the Greek only, never story
  events, reactions or wrong answers (see `CARD_TIPS` in `chapter1.js`).
- **Never name real apps or brands** (e.g. Duolingo). Say "her language app".
